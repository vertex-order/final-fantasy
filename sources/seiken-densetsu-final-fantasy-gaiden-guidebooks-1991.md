## Decisions
- Added as an entry (user, 2026-10-20): the two guidebooks count as comics on two sources saying there is manga in them (en.wikipedia for both, a Booklog reader review for the May book); the amount of manga is unknown, and the publisher texts list no manga chapter
- One entry with the two books as contents, like the XI Lightning Brigade guides
- Both books are in the entry (user, 2026-10-20): each has a reader remark of manga besides en.wikipedia (an Amazon JP review of 2004-03-10 says the August book has occasional funny manga and maker interviews; a Booklog review of 2009-06-03 says the May book has newly drawn manga); the amount in each is small or unknown, and the seller photos of six inner pages of the August book showed none
- One entry with the two books as volumes, placed right after Final Fantasy Adventure (both books are about that one game, so they are not split; no existing entry in group Other fits)
