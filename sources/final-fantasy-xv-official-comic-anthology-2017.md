# Final Fantasy XV Official Comic Anthology

Title: the wiki's English title, a literal rendering of ファイナルファンタジーXV 公式コミックアンソロジー (the "Official" is in the Japanese title; KADOKAWA calls it "唯一の公式アンソロジー", the only official anthology). Matches the Goodreads titles and the search terms.

Two volumes merged into one entry: Vol. 1 (2017-07-27) and Vol. 2 (2017-12-26); KADOKAWA's pages confirm both dates. `titleDate` = start of vol 1, end of vol 2. Rating, language links and platforms from vol 1.

## Author
