## Author

No `by` credit: an anthology of about 40 artists (see Sources); the serials' artists are named in the description's linked entries.

## Decisions

- Own entry with `contents[]` of the 7 volumes (user, 2026-10-16): it is a comic anthology magazine, not a guide: roughly 140 short comics in the seven issues plus serials (ja.wikipedia's contents); a different label and format from the Bros.comics EX line (sources/final-fantasy-xi-anthology-comic-2002.md), so not folded in
- Title "Final Fantasy XI ~Vana Digest~" is ours: bg-wiki's name; the printed title is コミックヴァナ通; parts are titled by the volume's feature, with each volume's on-sale date (the earlier of the two dates the page gives; NDL's month is the issue date's)
- The description ends with "Also included elsewhere: " and links the entries that hold its collected serials (user, 2026-10-16): Winds of Prayer (Manga), Claude and Haru (the Boy meets Girl series), Artist Collections (Ribbon)
- `profileUrl` is KADOKAWA's product search for コミックヴァナ通 (user, 2026-10-16), to show all seven volumes; the search is client-rendered, so curl cannot read the results; the user reports that it works (2026-10-16)
