## Editions (ISBN)

絵画の中の調査隊 (Kaiga no Naka no Chōsatai, "The Investigation Unit Within the Painting"); English title "Picturing the Past"; a short story, 15 chapters (per the fan translation's contents), printed in:
- Final Fantasy VII Remake World Preview (ファイナルファンタジーVII リメイク ワールドプレビュー), SE-mook, Square Enix, **2020-03-19**, AB size, 128 pages, 999 yen incl. tax, ISBN 978-4-7575-6541-8, https://magazine.jp.square-enix.com/gamebooks/books/10044 (page text pasted by the user 2026-10-17) -- date*, JA language*; the book is a preview and reference mook (characters, world, battle system, visual art, keywords); the story is its special feature
- Final Fantasy VII Remake: World Preview, English, Square Enix Books, **2020-09-22** (Goodreads and the publisher; the Final Fantasy Wiki says September 8), hardcover, 136 pages, ISBN 9781646090846 (ISBN10 1646090845), translated by Stephen Meyerink (per the wiki), Goodreads https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/52658168 (3.92 from 78 ratings), publisher page https://squareenixmangaandbooks.square-enix-games.com/en-us/product/9781646090846 (read by me 2026-10-17; it lists the book as an Art Book and does not mention the story) -- profileUrl*, EN language*, book platform (World Preview)*
- Final Fantasy VII Remake: Traces of Two Pasts, English, Square Enix Books, 2023-03-21, as **Episode 2 Coda, a retranslation by Stephen Kohler** (Final Fantasy Wiki, https://breezewiki.com/finalfantasy/wiki/Final_Fantasy_VII_Remake_Trace_of_Two_Pasts#Picturing_the_Past, pasted by the user 2026-10-17): the Special Survey Unit of the first translation became the Special Geological Survey Initiative, the narrator's name is revealed as Lonny and his mother's as Marielle in "Traces of Aerith", and the plot is unchanged -- book platform (Traces)*; whether the Japanese Traces carries it too is unconfirmed, but a reader's thread says Nojima added the story to the novel (see below), so it is likely

## Decisions

- Listed as its own entry, not merged into Traces of Two Pasts (user, 2026-10-17): it first appeared in the World Preview mook in 2020 and is reprinted in the English Traces, so the entry names both places to find it (two `book` rows) and the Traces entry's description mentions it; the same doubling-up pattern as elsewhere
- The World Preview book itself is a preview and reference mook and is not listed (see sources/vii-group.md)
- Reddit thread (page text pasted by the user 2026-10-17, https://www.reddit.com/r/FFVIIRemake/comments/19fhdwf/i_just_finished_final_fantasy_vii_remake_trace_of/): a reader of the English Traces finds the last chapter, "Episode 2: Coda", boring and confusing (generic unknown characters), and a reply says it was written for the 2020 release as Picturing the Past, "meant to highlight a bit of Aerith's past", and that "Nojima added that story into Traces of Two Pasts", so it feels disconnected from the rest of the novel; this corroborates the coda and suggests the story was added by the author, not only by the English publisher; the entry's description therefore says the novel includes it, without naming the edition
- Descriptions use the "Also included in" / "Also includes" pattern (user, 2026-10-17): this entry's second paragraph says "Also included in some editions of Traces of Two Pasts" and the Traces entry says "Some editions also include Picturing the Past" (user: "like 'some editions also include <anchor>'"; the hedge fits that the Japanese edition's contents are unconfirmed)
