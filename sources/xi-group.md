- **Vol.3 世界編 (電撃の旅団編 ヴァナ・ディール公式ワールドガイド Vol.3 世界編, 2005-09-30, ISBN 9784840229708) is not to be listed** (user, 2026-10-17): it has no four-panel comics, only maps and data (Amazon.co.jp reviews from 2005 say the 4-koma are missing and the book itself noted it); the reviews describe a guide of maps, monster data and quest information with Brigade columns but little story or prose, so with no comics to tip the scales it is out; it is one of the Dengeki Brigade guides looked up for the Lightning Brigade Guides entry and left out; the official MediaWorks catalogue page (Wayback, found 2026-10-17; see the Brigade guides sources) and the Communication Edition's page (「前回の「世界編」ではお休みだった４コママンガ」) confirm that it had no 4-koma; research and the reviews: [final-fantasy-xi-lightning-brigade-guides-2003.md](final-fantasy-xi-lightning-brigade-guides-2003.md)
- **Kept 2026-10-17 (user), within the context of the entire series:** the Encyclopedia is part 19 of the [Lightning Brigade Guides](final-fantasy-xi-lightning-brigade-guides-2003.md) entry, the last book of a long-running series the reader follows; it is in only as part of that series (with its 108 all-new four-panel comics), and on its own it would still not qualify, so the removal below stands for a standalone entry
- Lightning Brigade Encyclopedia (電撃の旅団公式十年事典, 2013) removed from the list as its own entry, 2026-10-13 (still true on its own; see above for its place in the series): it is a ten-year companion encyclopedia (jobs, NPCs, monsters, missions, developer interviews) with 108 scattered four-panel comics, not enough story or gag-comic content to warrant inclusion

- **Standing rule for art collections and event books (user, 2026-10-17):** art collections lean out, and are reconsidered only when they republish a whole manga series, not disjointed frames, covers or scattered pages from it; even then the book is an alternative source for that manga, recorded in the manga entry's description or as an alternate link there, not an "art compilation" entry of its own (as the Minagawa art book sits in the Love in the Sky entry); showing covers is not enough; a discussion or talk about a reading drama does not count, while a reading-drama performance or script might, depending on the drama's own content
