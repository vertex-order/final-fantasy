## Decisions

- ~Staying in Vana'diel~ (プレイ日記 : ヴァナ・ディール滞在記, "Play Diary : Staying in Vana'diel"), Enterbrain, May 2003, 477p;B6, 950 yen, ISBN 9784757714274 (bg-wiki 4-7577-1427-0), NDL 000004108136, goodreads https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/77429047 -- bg-wiki (https://www.bg-wiki.com/ffxi/Media,_Merchandise,_and_More/Publications/Books#Books): "The famous diaries that recorded an extraordinary number of hits on Famitsu.com has finally been published as a book. 270,000 words filled with laughter and tears about Final Fantasy XI. Stories from getting connected to the actual adventure, sprinkled with slang and etiquette. It's also great for beginners." -- removed from the list as a player's play diary and beginner's guide, not an adapted or original story, so outside the stories-only scope; confirmed by google books (https://books.google.ca/books?id=G73SPQAACAAJ: ファイナルファンタジー11プレイ日記: ヴァナ・ディール滞在記, author 永田泰大 (Nagata Yasuhiro), エンターブレイン, May 8, 2003, 477 pages; blurb: the Famitsu.com diary, 270,000 characters "from connecting to the net, the basics of adventuring, slang and etiquette... also ideal as a guide for beginners", with a later "bard edition" diary added) and amazon.co.jp (https://www.amazon.co.jp/dp/4757714270: authors 永田泰大 and みずしな孝之, tankobon hardcover, 4.7 of 5 from 83 ratings; blurb adds Mizushina's 4-panel manga and the bard-edition diary), so it is a real player's column and guide, not an in-world story
