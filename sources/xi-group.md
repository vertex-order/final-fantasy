## Decisions

- Lightning Brigade Encyclopedia (電撃の旅団公式十年事典, 2013) removed from the list, 2026-10-13: it is a ten-year companion encyclopedia (jobs, NPCs, monsters, missions, developer interviews) with 108 scattered four-panel comics, not enough story or gag-comic content to warrant inclusion

It's also great for beginners." -- removed from the list as a player's play diary and beginner's guide, not an adapted or original story, so outside the stories-only scope; confirmed by google books (https://books.google.ca/books?id=G73SPQAACAAJ: ファイナルファンタジー11プレイ日記: ヴァナ・ディール滞在記, author 永田泰大 (Nagata Yasuhiro), エンターブレイン, May 8, 2003, 477 pages; blurb: the Famitsu.com diary, 270,000 characters "from connecting to the net, the basics of adventuring, slang and etiquette... also ideal as a guide for beginners", with a later "bard edition" diary added) and amazon.co.jp (https://www.amazon.co.jp/dp/4757714270: authors 永田泰大 and みずしな孝之, tankobon hardcover, 4.7 of 5 from 83 ratings; blurb adds Mizushina's 4-panel manga and the bard-edition diary), so it is a real player's column and guide, not an in-world story
