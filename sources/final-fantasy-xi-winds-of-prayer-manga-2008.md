# Final Fantasy XI ~Winds of Prayer~ (Manga)

- Two-volume manga, one entry
- `titleDate` start: first serialization found (September 2008, Vana Digest Vol.01)
- `titleDate` end: last magazine issue found carrying it (March 2010, Vol.07; its cover still lists the artist among the serialized comics)
- Collected volumes are reprints; they don't move the dates
- The magazine ran seven volumes
  - NDL Search for コミックヴァナ通 lists only v.1 to v.7
  - Amazon Japan search for vol.08 returns no results (both as of 2026-10-09)
  - So Vol.07 is the last issue, and the manga's end is the magazine's end

## Decisions
- `profileUrl` is the KADOKAWA search above (user, 2026-10-16); `titleDate` stays 2008-09 to 2010-03, the serialization's start and end: with both known, every chapter was published by that end, and the volumes (2010-05-15 and 2010-11-15) are later editions of the same content, so they do not move the end (a wrong change to 2010-11-15 was made and reverted on 2026-10-16)
