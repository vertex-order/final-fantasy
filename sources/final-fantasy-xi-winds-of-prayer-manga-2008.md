# Final Fantasy XI ~Winds of Prayer~ (Manga)

Two-volume manga, one entry. The start is the first serialization found (September 2008, Vana Digest Vol.01) and the end is the last magazine issue found carrying it (March 2010, Vol.07, whose cover still lists the artist among the serialized comics); the collected volumes are reprints and do not move the dates. The magazine ran seven volumes: NDL Search for コミックヴァナ通 lists only v.1 to v.7 and an Amazon Japan search for vol.08 returns no results (both as of 2026-10-09), so Vol.07 is its last issue and the manga's end is the magazine's end.

## Author

Credits as printed in the books -- covers and first-page colophon of Vol.1 and Vol.2, from the eBay listing https://www.ebay.com/itm/315285686080 (scans kept locally in _reference/ffxi manga covers/, not committed) -- accessed 2026-10-09:
- 作画 沙月ゆう (cover and copyright line romanize it "You Satsuki") -- art
- 原作 スクウェア・エニックス (Square Enix) -- original work
- ストーリー はせがわみやび (Miyabi Hasegawa) -- story
- 小説版イラスト 金田榮路 -- illustrations of the novel version; the rare character 榮 is a reading from a scan, not cross-checked, so treat the spelling as provisional
- Vol.1 first printing 2010年5月27日, Vol.2 first printing 2010年11月26日; copyright (C) 2002-2010 Square Enix, (C) 2010 You Satsuki; publisher 発行人 浜村弘一, editor 編集人 青柳昌行 (partly cut off at the scan's bottom edge, reading provisional)

Reading confidence (scans read 2026-10-09): the roles 作画, 原作 and ストーリー, the names 沙月ゆう, スクウェア・エニックス and はせがわみやび, and both first-printing dates are large, clear text on the colophon page and repeat in small print on both covers (the novel cover and the Latin "Miyabi Hasegawa" agree), so they are corroborated across the images. The Vol.2 copyright line is cropped at the left edge and reads only "...-2010 SQUARE ENIX". The Latin romanization of the artist comes from the printed copyright line ("You Satsuki"), not from goodreads ("Yū Satsuki") or google books.

Entry `by`: "adapted by You Satsuki" -- the manga's creator (作画, and the adapter by the adapted-comic convention). The source novel's author, Miyabi Hasegawa (billed ストーリー on the covers), is not repeated in `by` or in the description: the description links the novel entry, which already credits her. Square Enix (原作) and the novel-version illustrator are not credited. Google Books' author fields for the two volumes (Vol.1: Hasegawa + Square Enix with Satsuki as illustrator; Vol.2: Satsuki only) are catalog inconsistencies; the printed credits are the primary source.
