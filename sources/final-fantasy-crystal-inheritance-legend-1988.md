# Final Fantasy: Crystal Inheritance Legend

## Author

- `by`: Osamu Mihara (三原治) and Shoko Uehara (上原尚子), no role (user, 2026-10-19): Mihara is credited 構成・文 on the cover, jacket and NDL; Uehara's role is not stated anywhere (copyright page "by", ja.wikipedia 著); Studio Hard (editing) and Mieko Watanabe (illustrations) are left out
  - Copyright page (English): "by Osamu Mihara, Shoko Uehara and Studio Hard Co.Ltd.", illustrations by Mieko Watanabe, "Character and Story Base Licence © SQUARE", first published by Keibunsha
  - NDL credits Mihara (構成・文) and Studio Hard (編); ja.wikipedia adds Uehara and Watanabe
  - Cover illustration: Yoshitaka Amano (jacket); not in `by`
