## Decisions
- `length` is `384p; H` (user, 2026-10-16): the official English edition, Square Enix Books hardcover, 384 pages (Goodreads https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/60556665-final-fantasy-vii-remake, read 2026-10-16), the English count being preferred
  - it was `406p; H`, the Japanese hardcover's count
- The self-published fan edition on Goodreads is not linked from the entry (user, 2026-10-17: translations are not linked directly; the entry carries a `fan-translation` search platform instead)
  - its second story, "The Investigation Unit Within the Painting", is identified (2026-10-17, pages pasted by the user): Kazushige Nojima's short story 絵画の中の調査隊 (Kaiga no Naka no Chōsatai), the official English title "Picturing the Past", first printed in the Final Fantasy VII Remake World Preview book (SE-mook, 2020-03-19, ISBN 978-4-7575-6541-8, 128 pages; English edition Square Enix Books 2020-09-08, ISBN 978-1-64609-084-6, 139 pages, translated by Stephen Meyerink)
  - a candidate for its own entry (see the VII sources for it, once written), and per the Final Fantasy Wiki the English Traces of Two Pasts reprints it as "Episode 2 Coda" (unchecked against the English edition)
- The English edition reprints the novella as "Episode 2 Coda: Picturing the Past" (Final Fantasy Wiki; retranslated by Stephen Kohler), so the description says the novel includes it (a Reddit thread, 2026-10-17, says Nojima added the story to the novel, so it is likely in the Japanese edition too, unconfirmed) and links the novella's own entry, final-fantasy-vii-remake-picturing-the-past-2020.md (user, 2026-10-17: list it separately and double up where to find it)
