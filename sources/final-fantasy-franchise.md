## Decisions

- **Not to be listed: Bravely Default: Flying Fairy (Manga) (user, 2026-10-17):** a 20-chapter, 4-volume manga of the 2012 DS game Bravely Default: Flying Fairy (Otapedia's description)
  - Bravely Default is a different Square Enix game series, unrelated to Final Fantasy, so its manga is out of scope
  - it appears in the data only as a mention in the description of Final Fantasy: The 4 Heroes of Light, whose sequel became that game
- **Not to be listed (user, 2026-10-17: agreed with the proposal), from the Otapedia books list:** Encyclopaedia Eorzea (an in-universe encyclopedia of Final Fantasy XIV lore; the kit scope excludes books that list what is in the game, same call as the XI Story Ultimania)
  - The Sky: The Art of Final Fantasy and Dawn: The Worlds of Final Fantasy (pure art books by Amano and others, art collections lean out)
  - the Final Fantasy Ultimania series (guidebooks and artbooks)
- **Not to be listed: the Final Fantasy Ultimania books, evaluated and rejected (user, 2026-10-17: we lean against strategy guides but note them as rejected so we know they were evaluated; one is included only when it holds a lot of comics or novellas):** the 20th Anniversary Ultimania (2008), the 25th Memorial Ultimania (2012), the Ultimania Archive volumes 1 to 3 (Dark Horse, 2018 onward) and the roughly 72 companion books are guidebooks, artbooks and reference books (character profiles, art, synopses, design notes), and the Otapedia page names no comics or novellas in them, so none is listed
  - the one Ultimania we looked at in detail, the XI Story Ultimania, is a named exclusion in xi-group.md
  - an individual Ultimania that turns out to hold comics or short stories is to be judged on its own
  - the Dark Horse page of Final Fantasy Ultimania Archive Volume 1 (https://www.darkhorse.com/Books/3000-773/Final-Fantasy-Ultimania-Archive-Volume-1) is rejected as a link for the same reason (user, 2026-10-17): it is one of the evaluated and rejected guidebook and artbook titles
- **Not to be listed: the Final Fantasy cafes (user, 2026-10-18):** Final Fantasy Eorzea Cafe (https://paselabo.pasela.co.jp/ff_eorzea/; Pasela, Akihabara 2014, Osaka 2017; XIV-inn decor, a few PCs; borderline) and SQUARE ENIX Cafe & Shop Shinjuku (https://paselabo.pasela.co.jp/sqex_cafe_shop/; multi-franchise, opened 2026-06-12). Themed restaurants with decor, menus and merchandise but no narrative or exclusive content count as merchandise. Background: https://otakumode.com/otapedia/games/final_fantasy/eorzea_cafe (stale, early 2020)
