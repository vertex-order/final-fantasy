## Decisions

- **Not to be listed: Bravely Default: Flying Fairy (Manga) (user, 2026-10-17):** a 20-chapter, 4-volume manga of the 2012 DS game Bravely Default: Flying Fairy (Otapedia's description); Bravely Default is a different Square Enix game series, unrelated to Final Fantasy, so its manga is out of scope; it appears in the data only as a mention in the description of Final Fantasy: The 4 Heroes of Light, whose sequel became that game
- **Not to be listed (user, 2026-10-17: agreed with the proposal), from the Otapedia books list:** Encyclopaedia Eorzea (an in-universe encyclopedia of Final Fantasy XIV lore; the kit scope excludes books that list what is in the game, same call as the XI Story Ultimania); The Sky: The Art of Final Fantasy and Dawn: The Worlds of Final Fantasy (pure art books by Amano and others, art collections lean out); the Final Fantasy Ultimania series (guidebooks and artbooks)
