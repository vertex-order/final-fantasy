- Japanese Wikipedia credits scenario 千葉広樹 for the earlier four-chapter 零式 manga only, not this series — accessed 2026-10-10
- Tetsuya Nomura (supervision) deliberately not credited: supervision is not a credit role here, and the source game's creator stays out of `by`
- Letterers and the translators (Alethea and Athena Nibley) deliberately not credited
