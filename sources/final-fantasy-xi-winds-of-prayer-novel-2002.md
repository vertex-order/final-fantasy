# Final Fantasy XI ~Winds of Prayer~ (Novel)

French and German editions were once a separate entry (~The Song of the Storm~ Vol.1). Merged: same book.

- Fan translations title it "The Song of the Storm/The Winds of Prayer"
- BnF says the French edition is translated from the Japanese
