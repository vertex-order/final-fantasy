# Tetra Master From Final Fantasy IX

## ja.wikipedia.org (Information)

- https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/テトラマスターFrom_FINALFANTASY_IX -- title*, dates*, platforms*, tags*, description* -- accessed 2026-10-28 (article read through the MediaWiki API, revision 95436216 of 2023-05-31); archived: TODO
  - Title テトラマスターFrom FINALFANTASY IX (TetraMaster From FINALFANTASY IX; short forms TM FFIX, TM FF9, テトラマスター)
  - An online game (MO) made from the card game クアッドミスト of Final Fantasy IX; service began 2002-05-16, as a title bundled with Final Fantasy XI; service ended 2010-12-31
  - Platforms: Windows and PlayStation 2; no standalone purchase, each copy comes inside another package
  - Packages: Final Fantasy XI; FFXI All-in Pack 2006; FRONT MISSION ONLINE; ソースネクスト はじめてのファイナルファンタジーXI (Windows only)
  - The PlayStation 2 version needs a hard disk drive
  - Cannot be played at the same time as Final Fantasy XI or Front Mission Online
  - Rules follow Quad Mist, with three-player matches and traps; matches against computer-controlled characters from Final Fantasy IX; card shop, auctions, rankings
  - A language-specific server is chosen first; no list of languages is given
  - Carries a notice asking for sources (2023-05)
