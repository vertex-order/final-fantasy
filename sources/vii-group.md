## Decisions

- Narrative order: VII (1997) leads, then The Maiden Who Travels The Planet, On the Way to a Smile, The Kids Are Alright, Advent Children, then the Remake/Rebirth/Revelation reimaginings -- per the community placement in the reddit.com thread above.
- Final Fantasy VII Remake World Preview (the preview and reference mook: characters, world, battle system, art, keywords; Japanese 2020-03-19, SE-mook, 128 pages, ISBN 978-4-7575-6541-8; English 2020-09-22, Square Enix Books, 136 pages, ISBN 9781646090846) is not listed (reason ours: a preview and reference book, outside the scope)
  - its Nojima short story is listed as [Picturing the Past](final-fantasy-vii-remake-picturing-the-past-2020.md)
- The cancelled 64DD version of Final Fantasy VII is not listed (user, 2026-11-03): announced for the Nintendo 64 disk drive add-on and never released; the game came out on PlayStation instead
  - Evidence: https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/NINTENDO_64のゲームタイトル一覧 (the 発売されなかったタイトル section: ファイナルファンタジーVII, スクウェア, 64DD, "PlayStationに移行。") and https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/64DD (the 移行して発売されたソフト section: released on PlayStation on 1997-01-31); details in sources/final-fantasy-vii-1997.md
- Not listed (user, 2026-11-08): books from the Final Fantasy Wiki's Square Enix Magazine & Book page (https://breezewiki.com/finalfantasy/wiki/Square_Enix_Magazine_%26_Book, accessed 2026-11-08) that are guidebooks, previews, artbooks, catalogues, magazines or development books, not entertaining on their own (docs/data.md, "What belongs on a list")
  - Dirge of Cerberus -Final Fantasy VII- Official Complete Guide — 2006-02-17 — ISBN 9784757516229
  - Final Fantasy VII series Special Book — 2020-01-31 — ISBN 9784757563681
- **Not to be listed: the cancelled SNES and Nintendo 64 versions of Final Fantasy VII (user, 2026-11-03):** work began on the SNES in 1994 and stopped when the staff finished Chrono Trigger (1995); early plans and technical tests followed for the Nintendo 64 and its 64DD add-on, then the game moved to PlayStation again (released 1997); no substantial work was finished, per Polygon's oral history (2017-01-09, https://www.polygon.com/a/final-fantasy-7); the SNES and Nintendo 64 are not platforms of Final Fantasy VII, as with the 64DD. Only these platforms are left out: the game itself released on PlayStation and stays listed on its released platforms
  - Evidence: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_cancelled_Nintendo_64_games (revision 1383021177 of 2026-10-31; Final Fantasy VII row); https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_cancelled_Super_NES_games (revision 1383237431 of 2026-11-01; Final Fantasy VII row); details in sources/final-fantasy-vii-1997.md
