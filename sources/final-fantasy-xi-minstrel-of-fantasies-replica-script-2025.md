# Final Fantasy XI ~Minstrel of Fantasies: Replica Script~

## Author

No `by` credit, per the user 2026-10-14: the scenarios are by 高橋玖 (three stories; the romanized reading is unconfirmed and unlikely to be found) and はせがわみやび (Miyabi Hasegawa, one story), with the script credited to 早川康介 and the direction to 小暮邦明 (dengekionline.com); a credit is left off when a name can't be romanized with confidence or only a director is known; as a staged reading drama (close to an audio drama) the performers would also need crediting, and the cast is too many names, so no `by`

## Editions (ISBN)

- No ISBN: bg-wiki gives a UPC

朗読劇 : 夢幻のウタイビト : 複製台本 (Reading Drama : Minstrel of Fantasies : Replica Script) — UPC 4942330370001, Japanese, Kadokawa, March 2025, 96pp, B5, 2530 yen, NDL N/A — per bg-wiki — JA language*, length (96p;B5)*, date (2025-03)*
- Includes a playbill with comments from the performers, writer, director and producer, and a scenario overview
- Digital version: bought through a physical card with a redemption code
- Four stories:
  - "Temple of the Heart's Secret" (Star Sibyl)
  - "Girl of the Underworld" (Aphmau)
  - "Blue Mage Blues" (Blue Mage)
  - "Memoirs of the Many-Headed Hydra -Memories of Heroes-" (Hydra Squadron)
