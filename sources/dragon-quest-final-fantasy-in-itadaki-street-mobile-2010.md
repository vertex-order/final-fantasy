## Square Enix Mobile (Publisher)

- https://web.archive.org/web/20110126184259/http://www.square-enix.co.jp/mobile/sem/itast-sp/ -- Final Fantasy in Itadaki Street Mobile (ファイナルファンタジー in いただきストリート MOBILE): title*, date*, platforms*, description* (capture of 2011-01-26) -- accessed 2026-10-24
  - Board game ("Itadaki Street"); Final Fantasy characters © Square Enix, © ARMOR PROJECT / Square Enix
  - Start 2010-07-01 on i-mode (NTT DoCoMo, FOMA903i and 703i series and later) and EZweb (au, WIN BREW); no Yahoo!ケータイ; 300 points a month (315 yen with tax); each added map 100 points (105 yen)
  - Launch roster: characters Cloud, Tifa, Yuffie (Final Fantasy VII) and a Moogle (Final Fantasy XI); maps Alexandria (IX), Tycoon Castle (V), Airship (series)
