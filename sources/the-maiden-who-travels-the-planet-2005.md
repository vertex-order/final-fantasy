- The Spanish language row is a fan translation (user, 2026-10-16): a self-published print (Independently published, 2018, ISBN 978-1977014238) of the translator's own version, with no Square Enix licence and no official publisher; it can be bought, so its Goodreads link stays; the tooltip reads "Spanish (fan translation; self-published print)", and `jpTag` on the book row stays since no official edition outside Japan exists
