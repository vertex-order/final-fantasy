## Audio drama (Fabula Nova Dramatica)

- Alpha (α): the Square Enix Music page (Wayback only, 2018-05-09): http://web.archive.org/web/20180509131833/http://www.square-enix.co.jp:80/music/sem/page/fabula/ff13/drama/ — profile*; the e-STORE product SQEX-10199 (Wayback 2014-03-22): http://web.archive.org/web/20140322212736/http://store.jp.square-enix.com/item/music/SQEX-10199 — disc platform*; "the important episodes leading up to the main game, newly written; the first three parts plus a bonus story only on the drama CD"; voiced by Maaya Sakamoto, Daisuke Ono and others — accessed 2026-10-11
  - (fetched)
- Both e-STORE pages (2026-10-11) confirm the dates: Alpha 2010年09月15日, Omega 2010年11月17日
  - genre ドラマCD each
  - the blurb calls the Omega "the second" (第二弾) drama CD
  - first pressings include a script-style booklet and an original sleeve case
  - each is "the three main episodes plus a drama-CD-only bonus story". Omega contents: FRIENDS (友達), PRESENT (贈物), TOMORROW (未来), and the bonus story DEPARTURE (出発)
  - Alpha's episode list isn't in the page text saved, only its bonus story REAL (現実)
- https://breezewiki.com/finalfantasy/wiki/Final_Fantasy_XIII_Episode_Zero_-Promise-_Fabula_Nova_Dramatica_Alpha — title link* (the Alpha version), length* (4 tracks), dates (September 15, 2010): tracks STRANGER -異邦-, TREASURE -家族-, SEARCH -検索-, REAL -現実- (bonus), Square Enix — accessed 2026-10-11
- https://breezewiki.com/finalfantasy/wiki/Final_Fantasy_XIII_Episode_Zero_-Promise-_Fabula_Nova_Dramatica_Omega — dates (November 17, 2010): tracks FRIENDS -友達-, PRESENT -贈物-, TOMORROW -未来-, DEPARTURE -出発- (bonus), 4 tracks, Square Enix — accessed 2026-10-11
- The wiki's #Drama_CD section also lists the January 27, 2010 bundled CD (seven short chapters, 1:43 to 5:50 each, from the soundtrack's limited edition, Part 1 of the web novellas)
  - the bundled CD's seven chapters and "Part 1" label belong to the soundtrack, not to the Alpha disc
- ja.wikipedia (the Final Fantasy XIII page, ドラマCD, fetched 2026-10-11): "a two-part (前・後編) drama CD that adds new chapters to the novel's content, a story continuing from the first episode on the first-edition soundtrack" — Alpha and Omega are the first and second halves, so the discs do not overlap
  - with episode 1 on the soundtrack, the three sets are 1 + 3 + 3 of the novella's seven parts
  - Omega's three are Friends, Present and Tomorrow, and the wiki's Alpha page confirms Alpha's three are Stranger, Treasure and Search
- Omega (Ω): e-STORE product SQEX-10210 (Wayback 2014-03-22): http://web.archive.org/web/20140322212710/http://store.jp.square-enix.com/detail/SQEX-10210 — listed as a related product on the Alpha page
  - no version of its own, its bonus story is named in the Alpha version's description with its release date
- The official page is a Flash site; a screenshot of it (2026-10-11) shows: "2010.9.15 On Sale", SQEX-10199
  - "the main three episodes plus a bonus story written only for the drama CD, 「REAL ―現実―」"
  - first pressing includes a script-style booklet and an original sleeve case
  - the Ω edition shows "11/17発売決定！"
  - the first-limited-edition FFXIII Original Soundtrack carries "Story01 -ENCOUNTER-" (the bundled CD, out of scope)
- Dates: Alpha September 15, 2010 (the official page above, also 4Gamer and the wiki's Drama CD section; the version's titleDate)
  - Omega November 17, 2010 (the wiki)
- Not covered: the January 27, 2010 CD bundled with the limited FFXIII Original Soundtrack (a soundtrack release)
