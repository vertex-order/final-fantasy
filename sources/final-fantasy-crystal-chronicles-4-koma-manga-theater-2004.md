# Final Fantasy Crystal Chronicles: 4-Koma Manga Theater

## Author

No `by` credit: it is an anthology by multiple artists (see below), so no single author applies.
- https://magazine.jp.square-enix.com/top/comics/detail/9784757511224/: no author field — accessed 2026-10-11 (fetched)
- https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/77600584: "Unknown Author" — accessed 2026-10-11 (page text pasted by user)
- A multi-artist anthology, confirmed 2026-10-14: the Japanese Wikipedia's hover text for "4コママンガ劇場" (the series article, text pasted by user, machine-translated) says "Four-frame Manga Theater" is a series of four-frame manga based on parody gags about video games and the like, "published in the form of an anthology by multiple artists from the 1990s to 2006", published by Square Enix (formerly Enix)
  - so no single `by` credit applies
- Colophon page (奥付) photographed by the user 2026-10-14 (seller photo from https://paypayfleamarket.yahoo.co.jp/item/z662130232, a PayPay Flea Market listing, which also gave the front cover image below): lists no artists, only the 編集 (editors) 安藤充謙, 松下正孝, 内野健志, 日浅浩民, 中川薫, 居場?? (partly cut off), 松崎武吏, 平本雄介, 菊池亜希子
  - 制作 (production) 田口浩司
  - ©2003 THE GAME DESIGNERS STUDIO, INC.
  - "CHARACTER DESIGN/Toshiyuki Itahana"
  - the facing page is a call for reader submissions (4-koma or short stories, 3 pages or 6 pages minimum, to the "4コママンガ劇場" desk at Square Enix's publishing division), and a fan-letter slip to write in a favourite artist's name -- both point to an anthology of many artists, but no page states it, and the strips' own signatures were not seen
- Front cover image provided by the user 2026-10-14 (seller photo): the title ファイナルファンタジー・クリスタルクロニクル 4コママンガ劇場 over art of the four tribes (Clavat, Selkie, Lilty, Yuke) and a moogle
  - no artist or author credit appears on the front, so the credit would be on the back cover or the colophon (奥付)
