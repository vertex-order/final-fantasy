# Final Fantasy XV: Comrades
<!-- site/data/group-XV.js, #entry-XV-final-fantasy-xv-comrades-2017 -->

## Author

None credited in the entry: a downloadable expansion by the Final Fantasy XV team (Square Enix); no writer or artist is named in the pages used.

## Editions

Final Fantasy XV Multiplayer: Comrades, previously Final Fantasy XV Multiplayer Expansion: Comrades (Japanese ファイナルファンタジーXV オンライン拡張パック：戦友, "Online Expansion Pack: Comrades")
- Original DLC: released **2017-11-15** (delayed from 2017-10-31 to optimize the experience); an online test for Season Pass owners ran August 3-8 and August 11-13, 2017; sold as a standalone DLC for $19.99, in the Season Pass, and built into Royal Edition (PlayStation 4 and Xbox One) and Windows Edition (PC), the Royal one with cross-platform co-op with Xbox One players when bought through the Microsoft Store
- Standalone version: December 2018 (revealed at PAX East 2018): ten new bosses and several other expansions; it replaced the original version on consoles (the original stopped working and its owners got the standalone free); a planned PC version was cancelled and the PC original kept working
- June 2020 online changes: en.wikipedia says online multiplayer was shut down in June 2020 (for Origin and Microsoft Store copies in the main game's article); **the primary source, Square Enix's notice of April 1, 2020 (effective June 24, 2020), is narrower**: on the PC Windows Edition it ends Origin matchmaking and cross-matching between the Steam and Origin versions (Comrades stays playable on Origin with NPC partners only), plus other online features (appearance change, other players' avatars, user and official treasures, user photos, the MOD Organizer online build); it lists no end of Steam-only matchmaking, and for the PlayStation 4 and Xbox One versions of the main game and of Comrades only the in-game INFORMATION (update news) item is removed
