# Final Fantasy XV: Comrades

## Author

- None credited: a downloadable expansion by the Final Fantasy XV team (Square Enix)

## Editions

Final Fantasy XV Multiplayer: Comrades, previously Multiplayer Expansion: Comrades (ファイナルファンタジーXV オンライン拡張パック：戦友)
- Original DLC: 2017-11-15 (delayed from 2017-10-31)
  - Sold alone, in the Season Pass, and built into the Royal Edition (PS4, Xbox One) and Windows Edition (PC)
- Standalone: December 2018 (North America 12-12, Europe and Japan 12-13)
  - Ten new bosses (one later), costumes, weapons
  - Replaced the original DLC on consoles; owners got it free
  - A planned PC standalone was cancelled; PC got the content through update 1.27 as Comrades version 2.00
- June 2020 online changes (Square Enix notice 2020-04-01)
  - Windows Edition: Origin matchmaking and Steam-Origin cross-matching ended; Comrades stays playable offline with NPCs
  - Consoles: only the in-game INFORMATION item removed
  - en.wikipedia's "online shut down" is broader than the notice
