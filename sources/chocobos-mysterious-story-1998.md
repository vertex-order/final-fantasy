## Editions (ISBN)

Shogakukan, てんとう虫コミックス, 6 volumes. Japanese only; no other-language edition found.

Goodreads has one page per volume and no series page; the author there is "Hiroshi Katou". Its "ladybug Comics" is a translation of てんとう虫コミックス.
