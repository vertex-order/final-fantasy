## Decisions

- `titleDate` is `{ start: '1990', end: '1992-09-04' }` (user, 2026-10-15): the start is the serialization in Maru Katsu Famicom (1990 vol.9 per ja.wikipedia, year only since issue dates are not given; the wiki and the recap blog say 1991), the end is the last volume's publisher date; the old 1991-01 had no source; the anchor and this file's name moved from 1991 to 1990
- `profileUrl` is the KADOKAWA search for 悠久の風伝説, which returns exactly the three volumes (confirmed by user in a browser)
- The `fan-recap` is the Marathon Recaps blog post on the manga (user, 2026-10-15), better than the wiki's #Story section, and it replaces the YouTube recap of the game, which is a different story
- The English translation on MangaDex and the 2008 e-book are not in the entry data (user, 2026-10-15)
- The JA language link is volume 1's Goodreads page (user, 2026-10-15), not the last volume's
- The entry has two book rows (user, 2026-10-15), told apart by `jpTag` (a plain book icon against one marked "(jp)"), since the page cannot show them as different icons and the sources file is for maintainers, never readers:
  - `Manga (Goodreads)`: the search `from Final Fantasy III Dragon Comics`, which returns exactly the three paper volumes and, through Goodreads, buy links at the reader's own Amazon store and currency (the international, English-friendly route; the volumes are out of print, so these are used copies);
  - `Manga (DuckDuckGo)` with `jpTag`: the search `悠久の風伝説 ファイナルファンタジーⅢより 衣谷遊 電子書籍 角川` (the user ran it), which returns the eBookJapan series page and volume 1, KADOKAWA's volume 1 product page, Rakuten Books, Amazon (used copies) and secondhand listings; it is the only path on the page to the Japanese e-book
  - not used: a Goodreads or Amazon icon for the Goodreads row (the `amazon` key is the Amazon App Store, and the link is a Goodreads search, not an Amazon page), a new magnifying-glass search icon (deferred; the platform icons live in the platforms repo), direct links to eBookJapan or the publisher's shop (regional retailers, Japanese only), and the archive.org scan (we do not link scans; the scan URLs stay in Sources as proof that the manga exists)
