## Author

No `by` credit: an anthology (about 17 to 19 artists per volume, many repeating), so no single author applies. The bg-wiki artist lists are romanized and inconsistent, so they are not transcribed.

## Decisions

- One entry for all 13 volumes (user, 2026-10-15): one imprint, an unbroken two-month run, one ISBN run; separate from the Bros anthology (different publisher and imprint; sources/final-fantasy-xi-anthology-comic-2002.md)
- Story and four-panel mix goes in the story column (user, 2026-10-15): the blurbs mix story comics and gags, and story outweighs comedy
- Volume 5 (4-Panel Manga Laugh Stadium) stays in the list (user, 2026-10-15): it is in the numbered run with the same ISBN run and bimonthly cadence, though its blurb is pure 4-koma
- Title "Final Fantasy XI ~Ozora Anthology Comic~" is ours (user, 2026-10-15): no official series name found; the volumes print only FINAL FANTASY XI (publisher) / アンソロジーgameコミックス (Amazon, Goodreads)
