## Decisions

- Not in the Final Fantasy XI Novels section; stays in the main section
  - Same author and subject, but a Kadokawa B6 book 2015, not the Enterbrain Famitsu Bunko line (last entry there 2009)
  - Cover has no volume number or series mark
  - bg-wiki and NDL both frame it as a new, original, definitive novelization, not a next volume
  - "Continuation" in the blurb is the game's timeline (after the Escafication crisis), not the earlier novels' characters
  - Not found on LibraryThing's series list or in the ja.wikipedia protagonist grouping
  - Revisit if a source ties it to Al & Iris or Doug & Lin
