## Decisions

- Section title: LibraryThing's English series name, "Final Fantasy XI Novels"
  - "Official" left out of the title on purpose
  - Not "Famitsu Bunko": that imprint also carries unrelated books, e.g. the XIV novel
- Series numbers ("Book N"): the position in the author's release-ordered list, oldest first
  - Not printed on the page, derived by counting; cross-checked three ways, no conflict
    - LibraryThing: Vol.1-10 and 13 match books 1-10 and 13
    - Goodreads German series: Bd 1-10 match books 1-10
    - ja.wikipedia: Minagawa illustrates volumes 25 and 30, Kaneda, Minagawa and Uzu volume 29; these are Happy Gift, the first Heartbeat of Xarcabard and the Anthology
    - French Fleuve Noir covers: Tome 1-4 print "1" to "4" in the top right corner, matching Books 1-4; Tome 5 and 6 are numbered in their titles only
  - Multi-volume entries take a range; "Vol." stays the label for the parts inside one entry
  - 1 Winds of Prayer; 2 Star Oath; 3 Eternal Bond; 4-6 Sword of Protection; 7 Faraway Wings; 8-10 Pride of the Knight
  - 11 Adventurer's Holiday; 12-13 A Distant Wish; 14-15 A New Dream; 16 Blessings of the Journey; 17-19 Daughters of Aht Urhgan
  - 20-21 Testament of the Wise; 22 Conditions of Fortune; 23 Princess Knight of Ronfaure; 24 The Star's Call; 25 Happy Gift
  - 26-28 A Message from Beyond; 29 Anthology; 30-32 Heartbeat of Xarcabard
  - German titles for books 1-10: Das Lied des Sturms, Der Sternenschwur, Der Bund der Ewigkeit, Das Schwert des Wächters 1-3, Die Flügel der Ferne, Der Stolz der Paladine 1-3
- Descriptions are premise only, no spoilers
  - Multi-volume entries describe the opening setup of Vol.1 only; later-volume plots are left out
  - Nothing that reveals how an earlier book ended, or events after the opening of the entry
  - Audited 2026-10-12: Star Oath (Gelsba tank), Eternal Bond, Sword of Protection, Pride of the Knight, A Distant Wish, A New Dream, Daughters of Aht Urhgan, Testament of the Wise, The Star's Call, A Message from Beyond, Heartbeat of Xarcabard trimmed
- Members: the 18 entries in the ja.wikipedia protagonist grouping, all Hasegawa novels, plus the Anthology and the Winds of Prayer manga
  - The Anthology is Book 29 (three authors: Hasegawa, Takadono Madoka, Taguchi Sennendō, per the author's page), moved in because it is a numbered volume
  - The manga adapts the first novel, so it sits with the series it comes from
  - Doug & Lin: Sword of Protection, Pride of the Knight, Blessings of the Journey, A Message from Beyond
  - Al & Iris: Winds of Prayer, Star Oath, Eternal Bond, Faraway Wings, Adventurer's Holiday, A Distant Wish, A New Dream, Testament of the Wise, Conditions of Fortune, Princess Knight of Ronfaure, The Star's Call
  - Pasha & Nelly: Daughters of Aht Urhgan
  - Larry & Bey: Happy Gift
  - Windurst missions: Heartbeat of Xarcabard
- Left in the main section:
  - Songs of the Endless Earth: Kadokawa 2015, unnumbered cover, blurb says new original adventure; see its own sources file
  - Both Minstrel Replica Scripts: other format, not part of the numbered series
  - The Out of Orders (manhwa), Adventure Log (webcomic), the other manga and the Lightning Brigade Encyclopedia: not novels or adaptations of them
