# Final Fantasy XIV: I Will Teach You Professor Lalafell

Title: the official English title as given by the fandom wiki (printed inside the book, per the wiki) and a second fan-translation listing, "Final Fantasy XIV - I Will Teach You Professor Lalafell". Literal gloss of the Japanese: "Lalafell-sensei Will Teach You!". The earlier working title "Lalafell-sensei's Gonna Teach Ya!" has no web results and was dropped.

Three volumes (complete) in one entry: vol 1 (2017-08-26) to vol 3 (2018-11-26). `titleDate` is the serialization in 月刊デンプレコミック (see Decisions), not the volumes.

## Decisions

- `titleDate` is `{ start: '2017-01-26', end: '2018-08', approx: true }` (user, 2026-10-16): the serialization in 月刊デンプレコミック (電撃PlayStation's monthly supplement), not the volumes; the start is the January 2017 issue (episode 1; the artist's post of 2017-01-26 says it has started, his post of 2017-02-24 calls the next one the second episode, the December 2016 notice lists none), the day being the issue date by our arithmetic (631号, probably 2017-01-26); the end is August 2018, the last episode being 18 (2018-07-27, #20) or 19 (#21, 2018-08-28), as none of 668号 to 670号 lists one and the August notice was not found; the volumes (2017-08-26 to 2018-11-26) do not move the range, per docs/data.md "Native release is the authoritative date"
