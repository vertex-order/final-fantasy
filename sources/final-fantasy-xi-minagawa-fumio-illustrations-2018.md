  - 1 冒険者の章: illustrations for magazine features and walkthrough articles
  - 2 NPCの章: the story's NPCs
  - 3 アイドル戦士ミュモルの章: official art of the Idol Warrior Mumor from the summer event
  - 4 恋の空蝉1・2・3の章: every episode and an extra episode of the manga 恋の空蝉1・2・3, serialized in the mook ヴァナ・ディール通信, with character art (the "Love in the Sky 1·2·3!" that bg-wiki's 5th Anniversary Memoirs entry mentions)
  - 5 ノベライズ作品の章: the cover and interior illustrations Minagawa made for Hasegawa's FFXI novels
  - 6 エトセトラの章: merchandise art, Vana TV art, comic works and a setting-reference relationship chart
  - 7 ラフスケッチの章: character design drawings for the novels and rough sketches
