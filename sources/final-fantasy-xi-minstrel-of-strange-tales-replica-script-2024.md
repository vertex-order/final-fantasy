# Final Fantasy XI ~Minstrel of Strange Tales: Replica Script~

## Author

No `by` credit, per the user 2026-10-14: the stories were written by unnamed former FFXI scenario staff (Kadokawa's page), and the only named credit is the director (演出 早川康介 Kosuke Hayakawa), which is not an author; a book with only a director or too many authors gets no attribution line; as a staged reading drama (close to an audio drama) the performers would also need crediting, and the cast is too many names, so no `by`

## Editions (ISBN)

- No ISBN: bg-wiki gives a UPC

朗読劇 : 異聞のウタイビト : 複製台本 (Reading Drama : Minstrel of Strange Tales : Replica Script) — UPC 4942330227312, Japanese, Kadokawa, February 2024, 96pp, B5, 2530 yen, NDL N/A — per bg-wiki — JA language*, length (96p;B5)*, date (2024-02)*
- Includes a playbill with comments from the performers, writer, director and producer, and a scenario overview
- Performed live on 2024-02-17 (playonline); the book is the script of that performance
- Five tales:
  - "Mimiroon's Sparkling Great Adventure"
  - "Go For It! Allied Forces of Altana : Extreme Survival"
  - "Seaside Serpent Taro"
  - "A Small Gift For A Small Friend"
  - "Black Night Butterfly"
