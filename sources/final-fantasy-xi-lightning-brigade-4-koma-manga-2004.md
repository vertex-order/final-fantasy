## Decisions

- No longer flagged `helpWanted`: both volumes looked up; credit, language and links are NO.1's
- The entry uses NO.1's Goodreads page for the rating and the Japanese language link
- NO.1 (2004) and NO.2 (2007) are one series, so one entry with a "2 volumes" length
  - `titleDate` runs from NO.1's day (2004-02-20) to NO.2's day (2007-03-23), both from KADOKAWA and Amazon.co.jp
  - NDL and bg-wiki give 2004.3 and 2007.4: probably the cataloging month, as elsewhere
- Pages, not shown while the entry is a two-volume length
  - NO.1: 257 (NDL, Amazon.co.jp, bg-wiki) over 256 (KADOKAWA, Google Books)
  - NO.2: 352 (KADOKAWA, Amazon.co.jp, Google Books) over 353 (NDL, bg-wiki)
  - Rule: the figure most records agree on
- Title is our translation: "Lightning Brigade 4-Koma Manga" for 電撃の旅団編 えふえふいれぶん・4コマ・マンガ
- Description wording from the Amazon.co.jp blurb; no strip count given since it is only NO.1's (312)
- Added 2026-10-13 as the Dengeki Brigade's gag-comic collections, in place of the Lightning Brigade Encyclopedia (see final-fantasy-xi-lightning-brigade-encyclopedia-2013.md)
