# Final Fantasy XI ~Lightning Brigade Guides~
<!-- site/data/group-XI.js, #entry-XI-final-fantasy-xi-lightning-brigade-guides-2003 -->

The Dengeki Brigade (電撃の旅団) guide books for Final Fantasy XI (and the XIV ones found in the same search).

## Scope

Mediaworks / ASCII Media Works / KADOKAWA books by 電撃プレイステーション編集部 and the Dengeki Brigade (電撃の旅団), the Dengeki PlayStation magazine's Final Fantasy XI team. The question is which of them carry enough comics to be listed (docs/data.md "Mixed books": a guide that carries original comics is borderline, decided per book; strategy guides are not listed). Known: 電撃の旅団編 Vol.1 holds 166 all-new four-panel manga (see Vol.1 below); the strips of Vol.1 and Vol.2 are translated in the Dengeki 4-Koma International entry.

## How the books connect (our reading)

- All are by 電撃プレイステーション編集部 as the Dengeki Brigade (電撃の旅団), the magazine's FFXI team: the numbered and expansion "Vana'diel Official World Guides" (Vol.1, Vol.2, the Vol.2.6 supplement, Vol.3 in three parts, the expansion and jobs guides, the yearly 2010 to 2012 guides), the video strategy series 1 and 2, the Fashion books, the Chronicle compilations, the Decennial Encyclopedia, and an official doujinshi

## Decisions

- One entry with `contents[]` for the guides that qualify, so one sources file for all of them (user, 2026-10-16): the books are looked up as if listed, one at a time, and decided as a whole afterwards; strategy guides are not listed by default (docs/data.md), so only guides whose comics are a body of content are in
- Vol.1's author line: 電撃プレイステーション編集部 is an editorial credit, not an artist, so a listed entry would carry no `by` (ours)
- Sibling rule (user, 2026-10-16): if one volume of a similar set has comics, assume the others do, as long as they are marketed similarly; so クロニクル 1 is assumed to carry strips like クロニクル 2 (both marketed as compilations of the Brigade's magazine articles; クロニクル 2's own evidence is one reader comment, so both stay "likely, no count" in the sources), and the two fashion books, whose siblings carry none and whose descriptions list none, are assumed to have none; Vol.3 世界編 is assumed to have none (its cover had no strip box, unlike the other Vol.3 books)
- Vol.2.6, the 68-page supplement of Dengeki PlayStation issue 288 with 62 strips, is included in the roll-up as another part (user, 2026-10-16)
- The Official Doujinshi (2012) is excluded: a fan question-and-answer book, though its back cover carries a small manga page (user, 2026-10-16)
- Chronicle 1 stays in the `contents[]` series (user, 2026-10-16): its reprinted May to June 2002 pages include the start of the 4-koma; Chronicle 2, the same compilation, goes with it (ours, from the sibling rule; the user spoke of Chronicle 1)
- Why the series belongs on the list, though its strategy guides alone would not (user, 2026-10-16): it is long-running (2003 to 2013), and the Brigade is a group the reader follows and identifies with, characters with personalities brought to life by the four-panel comics, in writing that is often "we did this quest, here is what happened" more than pure storytelling; so the entry stands for the Brigade's story and strips, and the description says it is part strategy guide, part account of their adventures and part four-panel manga, often humorous
- One rolled-up entry, not broken out by story arc (user, 2026-10-16): unlike the XI novel series, which is pure storytelling in separate arcs and has an entry per book, the Brigade's books are one continuing group the reader follows, with no arcs to separate, so the whole run is one entry with `contents[]`
