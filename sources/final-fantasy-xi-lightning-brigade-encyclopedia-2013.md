## Decisions

- Length 217p, not 216
  - 217 pages: NDL, Google Books, Amazon.com, bg-wiki
  - 216 pages: KADOKAWA, Amazon.co.jp (both publisher-fed)
  - Rule: use the figure most records agree on; publisher pages can include front matter
- Date 2013-03-12 from the KADOKAWA page, over the month-only dates elsewhere
- Description: no number for the comics ("scattered four-panel comics")
  - bg-wiki's "four original comics" is a mistranslation of 描き下ろし4コマ
  - Dengeki Online counts 108 strips
- Removed from the list, 2026-10-13: not enough story content to warrant inclusion
  - It is a ten-year companion encyclopedia, not a story
    - About 176 of its 216 pages are reference: Job Archives (80), NPC encyclopedia (31), monster encyclopedia (17), story and mission encyclopedia (38, a summary of missions), glossary (10)
    - The rest is the game's history, Adoulin news and two developer interviews: behind the scenes, which the list leaves out
  - The 108 four-panel comics (描き下ろし4コマ) are scattered through the reference pages, not a collection read together
    - Enough strips together can stand in for a gag-comic volume (about one Garfield-sized volume's worth), but these aren't together
  - Guides, encyclopedias and lists of NPCs, monsters and missions are out of scope
  - The tags `Manga` and `Short Stories` it carried had no source behind them
  - The Brigade's own four-panel books were added instead as placeholder entries; see the XI group sources (xi-group.md)
- **Kept 2026-10-17 (user), within the context of the entire series:** the Encyclopedia is part 19 of the Lightning Brigade Guides entry (sources/final-fantasy-xi-lightning-brigade-guides-2003.md), the last book of a long-running series the reader follows; it is in only as part of that series, with its 108 all-new four-panel comics; the "Removed from the list" decision above still holds for it as a standalone entry (an encyclopedia, strips scattered through reference pages), so it is not listed on its own
