# Chocobo World

## breezewiki.com (Information; Final Fantasy Wiki mirror)

- https://breezewiki.com/finalfantasy/wiki/Chocobo_World -- story*, platforms*, release dates*, titleUrl* (text pasted by user) -- accessed 2026-10-23; archived: TODO
  - Intro: a PocketStation game compatible with Final Fantasy VIII; the player controls Boko the Chicobo on a quest to find MiniMog, assisted by Moomba and Cactuar
  - PocketStation never released outside Japan, so international PlayStation owners could not play it; the connection to the main game was programmed into every localization of Final Fantasy VIII, so it is playable with a PocketStation bought from Japan
  - Ported and included in the PC version of Final Fantasy VIII, including the 2013 re-release, where it is playable from the launcher after booting up the game
  - Removed from Final Fantasy VIII Remastered; the exclusive items can still be obtained via Angelo Search
  - Release dates: PocketStation Japan 1999-02-11 (also listed: United States/Canada 1999-09-09, Europe/Australia 1999-10-27); PC United States/Canada 1999-12-31, Japan 2000-03-23; Steam Europe/United States/Canada 2013-12-05, Asia 2013-12-12, Japan 2014-05-08
- https://breezewiki.com/finalfantasy/wiki/Chocobo_World#Story -- story* (text pasted by user) -- accessed 2026-10-23; archived: TODO
  - Boko, MiniMog, Moomba and Cactuar are friends; MiniMog goes alone to find a treasure in Scary Mountain and does not return, and the other three set out to rescue it, Boko leading, Moomba searching for weapons, Cactuar for items
  - The rest of the section is a spoiler and is not used
- https://breezewiki.com/finalfantasy/wiki/Chocobo_series -- the series page's short entry for the game (text pasted by user) -- accessed 2026-10-23
  - Chocobo World (おでかけチョコボRPG, Odekake Chokobo RPG), a PocketStation minigame released as part of Final Fantasy VIII; Boko, a baby chocobo, wanders a nondescript landscape in search of "events" such as enemy battles
  - Some items obtained in it, such as the Moomba summon, could not be accessed overseas; ported to the PC version of Final Fantasy VIII in 2000 and released in North America
