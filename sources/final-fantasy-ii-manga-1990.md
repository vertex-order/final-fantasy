## Decisions

- `titleDate` is an approximate 1990, `{ start: '1990', approx: true }` (user, 2026-10-15): the FFII manga was begun after the FFI book (30 December 1989) and partly printed in a Famicom Hisshobon supplement of unknown date
  - the old 1989-12 had no source
  - the anchor and this file's name moved from 1989 to 1990
