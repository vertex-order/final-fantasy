Where to start? ¶

You can play Final Fantasy in any order — no way is more correct than another. Ours is just one take; feel free to modify it and make it your own.

New players ¶

If you've never played a Final Fantasy game, or a turn-based RPG, starting with the action-oriented Final Fantasy XVI (2023) is a great entry point. Come back here when you're done.

A few games ¶

If you've only played a few, then there's a few good places to go next.

Pixels; Love pixel art? Start at the beginning with Final Fantasy (1987) and keep going in narrative order.

3D; 3D your jam? The three-part Final Fantasy VII: Remake Intergrade (2021), Rebirth, and Resonance will get your feet wet, then either keep going or go back to Final Fantasy III: 3D Remake (2006).

Realtime; Prefer more realtime-ish action titles? Final Fantasy XVI (2023) and Final Fantasy XV (2016) have you covered.

Social; Want a great story in a social setting with others? Final Fantasy XIV: Online (2013) begins one of the series' grandest adventures.

Many games ¶

If you've played many Final Fantasy titles already, why not start from the beginning and go series by series? Chronological order works well once you've already played the mainline entries and want to explore additional material.
