# Wiki corrections

This list tracks places where a wiki page (or another community-edited
catalog) disagrees with the evidence we found while researching an entry.
Our entries follow the better-sourced value; this file records the wiki's
value, ours, and why, so the corrections can be reported upstream.

- One item per wiki page and fact.
- Each item gives the page URL, the wrong or missing value, the corrected
  value, our source document (which holds the full evidence), and a
  one-line reason.
- **Confirmed:** we checked it against a primary or publisher record.
  **Candidate:** it looks wrong but is not settled; do not report it yet.
- Add an item whenever a sources file records that a wiki "is wrong".
- How to maintain this file: [sources.md](sources.md), section "Wiki corrections".

## Confirmed

### Final Fantasy VII: On the Way to a Smile (English edition length)

- Page: https://breezewiki.com/finalfantasy/wiki/Final_Fantasy_VII:_On_the_Way_to_a_Smile
- Says: 208 pages (English edition, Yen Press, 2018).
- Should be: 204 pages.
- Our source: [final-fantasy-vii-on-the-way-to-a-smile-2005.md](../sources/final-fantasy-vii-on-the-way-to-a-smile-2005.md)
- Backed by: the Yen Press product page lists 204 pages
  (https://yenpress.com/titles/9781975382353-final-fantasy-vii-on-the-way-to-a-smile).

### Final Fantasy XIII-2: Fragments After (English edition length)

- Page: https://breezewiki.com/finalfantasy/wiki/Final_Fantasy_XIII-2_Fragments_After

### Final Fantasy VII Remake Trace of Two Pasts (page counts and English date)

- Page: https://breezewiki.com/finalfantasy/wiki/Final_Fantasy_VII_Remake_Trace_of_Two_Pasts
- Says: 288 pages (Japanese), 373 pages (English), English release 20 March 2023.
- Should be: 406 pages (Japanese), 384 pages (English), English release 21 March 2023.
- Our source: [final-fantasy-vii-remake-traces-of-two-pasts-2021.md](../sources/final-fantasy-vii-remake-traces-of-two-pasts-2021.md)
- Backed by: the NDL record of the Japanese edition (406p, ISBN 978-4-7575-7348-2) and the
  Square Enix Books page of the English edition (384 pages, March 21, 2023)
  (https://squareenixmangaandbooks.square-enix-games.com/product/9781646091775).

### Wikipedia: List of Final Fantasy media (The Out of Orders date and ISBN)

- Page: https://wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Final_Fantasy_media#Novels_and_manga
- Says: Final Fantasy XI ~The Out of Orders~ released April 19, 2003, JP ISBN
  978-4-7577-1424-3.
- Should be: serialized from December 2004; the date and ISBN belong to the
  Star Oath novel (4-7577-1424-6), whose row lists the same ISBN.
- Our source: [final-fantasy-xi-the-out-of-orders-2004.md](../sources/final-fantasy-xi-the-out-of-orders-2004.md)
- Backed by: Square Enix's archived back-number page puts issue 1 on sale
  2004-12-03; the Star Oath is dated 2003-04-19 in
  [final-fantasy-xi-the-star-oath-2003.md](../sources/final-fantasy-xi-the-star-oath-2003.md).

### Tokyo Otaku Mode Otapedia: Derivative Manga (Figaro no Kekkon game)

- Page: https://otakumode.com/otapedia/games/final_fantasy/manga
- Says: Figaro no Kekkon: Tales from Desert is a doujinshi "based on Final Fantasy VII".
- Should be: Final Fantasy VI (it is about Sabin, Edgar and Figaro Castle).
- Our source: [figaros-wedding-tales-from-desert-1994.md](../sources/figaros-wedding-tales-from-desert-1994.md)

## Candidates

### Square Enix Magazine & Book (Final Fantasy XII manga volume dates)

- Page: https://breezewiki.com/finalfantasy/wiki/Square_Enix_Magazine_%26_Book#Manga
- Says: volume II August 22, 2008, volume III April 22, 2008.
- Probably: volume II is dated wrongly (it should come before III).

- Reason: volume order disagrees with the release dates; no catalog record
  checked yet.

### Final Fantasy XI The Out of Orders (start date is the cover date)

- Page: https://breezewiki.com/finalfantasy/wiki/Final_Fantasy_XI_~The_Out_of_Orders~
- Says: started in issue 1, December 17, 2004.
- Probably: issue 1 went on sale December 3, 2004; December 17 is the issue
  date printed on the cover (the wiki's date is not wrong as an issue date,
  but other pages list on-sale dates).
- Our source: [final-fantasy-xi-the-out-of-orders-2004.md](../sources/final-fantasy-xi-the-out-of-orders-2004.md)
- Reason: Square Enix's archived back-number page for the first issue
  (創刊号【2004.12.17号】) says 2004年12月3日発売; candidate until the wiki's
  own convention (cover date or on-sale date) is known.

### Final Fantasy II Shōri e no Tabidachi (author missing)

- Page: https://breezewiki.com/finalfantasy/wiki/Final_Fantasy_II_Sh%C5%8Dri_e_no_Tabidachi
- Says: no author listed.
- Probably: add the author, Naoko Uehara (上原尚子), per a podcast list and a
  retailer's editor credit; wikiwiki.jp names Tatsumi Yamashita instead.
- Our source: [final-fantasy-ii-journey-to-victory-1988.md](../sources/final-fantasy-ii-journey-to-victory-1988.md)
- Reason: no primary catalog names a writer, so report it only after a
  primary record is found.
