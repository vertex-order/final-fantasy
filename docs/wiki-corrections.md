# Wiki corrections

This list tracks places where a wiki page (or another community-edited
catalog) disagrees with the evidence we found while researching an entry.
Our entries follow the better-sourced value; this file records the wiki's
value, ours, and why, so the corrections can be reported upstream.

- One item per wiki page and fact.
- Each item gives the page URL, the wrong or missing value, the corrected
  value, our source document (which holds the full evidence), and a
  one-line reason.
- **Confirmed:** we checked it against a primary or publisher record.
  **Candidate:** it looks wrong but is not settled; do not report it yet.
- Add an item whenever a sources file records that a wiki "is wrong".
- How to maintain this file: [sources.md](sources.md), section "Wiki corrections".

## Confirmed

### Final Fantasy VII: On the Way to a Smile (English edition length)

- Page: https://breezewiki.com/finalfantasy/wiki/Final_Fantasy_VII:_On_the_Way_to_a_Smile
- Says: 208 pages (English edition, Yen Press, 2018).
- Should be: 204 pages.
- Our source: [final-fantasy-vii-on-the-way-to-a-smile-2005.md](../sources/final-fantasy-vii-on-the-way-to-a-smile-2005.md)
- Backed by: the Yen Press product page lists 204 pages
  (https://yenpress.com/titles/9781975382353-final-fantasy-vii-on-the-way-to-a-smile).

### Final Fantasy XIII-2: Fragments After (English edition length)

- Page: https://breezewiki.com/finalfantasy/wiki/Final_Fantasy_XIII-2_Fragments_After
- Says: 224 pages (English edition, Yen Press, 2019).
- Should be: 146 pages.
- Our source: [final-fantasy-xiii-2-fragments-after-2012.md](../sources/final-fantasy-xiii-2-fragments-after-2012.md)
- Backed by: the Yen Press product page lists 146 pages
  (https://yenpress.com/titles/9781975382384-final-fantasy-xiii-2-fragments-after).

### Goodreads: Final Fantasy XI Lightning Brigade 4-Koma Manga NO.2 (author)

- Page: Goodreads record for ISBN 9784840238199 (a community-edited catalog, not a wiki).
- Says: author "Satoshi Emoto".
- Should be: Hijiri Emoto (江本 聖).
- Our source: [final-fantasy-xi-lightning-brigade-4-koma-manga-2004.md](../sources/final-fantasy-xi-lightning-brigade-4-koma-manga-2004.md)
- Backed by: the NDL authority record reads 江本聖 as エモト ヒジリ; the
  Japanese Wikipedia, KADOKAWA and Amazon.co.jp all print the same
  characters.

  checked yet.
